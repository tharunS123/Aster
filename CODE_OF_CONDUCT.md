# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want Aster to be a welcoming project for everyone who takes part, whatever their age, body, disability, ethnicity, gender identity or expression, experience, nationality, appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation. Aster is a small beta project with a small number of people, so a little courtesy goes a long way.

## What we expect

- Be kind and patient, especially with people new to macOS or to reporting bugs.
- Give feedback on the work, not the person. Be specific and constructive.
- Assume good intent, and say so when you're unsure.
- Respect privacy: don't post other people's personal information, and don't paste your own dictated text or meeting content into issues.
- Say what you actually observed. Don't overstate what works.

## What we don't accept

- Harassment, insults, or personal or political attacks.
- Sexualized language or imagery, or unwelcome attention.
- Publishing someone else's private information without permission.
- Repeatedly pushing for changes after a maintainer has declined them with a reason.
- Other conduct that would reasonably be seen as inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope

This applies in the repository's issues, discussions, and any space where someone represents the project.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, close or lock threads, and block people who break these rules. To report a problem, contact the maintainer privately through the email on their GitHub profile ([@tharunS123](https://github.com/tharunS123)). Reports are kept confidential. Maintainers will respond as soon as they can and explain any decision.
